TailwindCSS provides several utility classes for handling `<img>` tags. Here are some of the most commonly used classes: 
- `object-contain`: This class sets the image to maintain its aspect ratio while fitting within the container. The image will shrink or enlarge as needed to ensure it fits the container. 
- `object-cover`: This class sets the image to maintain its aspect ratio and cover the entire container. It will crop or shrink the image as needed to ensure it covers the container. 
- `object-fill`: This class sets the image to fill the entire container, but does not maintain its aspect ratio. This may result in the image being stretched or compressed to fit the container. 
- `object-none`: This class sets the image to its natural size without any adjustments or cropping.
- `object-center`: This class centers the image horizontally and vertically within its container. 
- `object-left`: This class aligns the image to the left side of its container.
- `object-right`: This class aligns the image to the right side of its container. 
- `object-top`: This class aligns the image to the top of its container.
- `object-bottom`: This class aligns the image to the bottom of its container.

These classes can be combined with other TailwindCSS classes to further style your images, such as setting specific widths or heights, adding borders or shadows, adjusting opacity, and more.
