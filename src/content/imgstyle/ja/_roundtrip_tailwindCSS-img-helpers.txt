TailwindCSS offers several helper classes for manipulating the `<img>` tag. Here are the most commonly used classes:

- `object-contain`: This class ensures the image fits within the container while maintaining its aspect ratio. The image is scaled down or up to fit within the container.
- `object-cover`: This class makes the image cover the entire container while maintaining its aspect ratio. It trims or resizes the image to cover the entire container.
- `object-fill`: This class makes the image fill the entire container and does not maintain the aspect ratio. This may stretch or compress the image to ensure it fits the container.
- `object-none`: This class sets the image to its natural size without resizing or trimming.
- `object-center`: This class centers the image horizontally and vertically within the container.
- `object-left`: This class positions the image on the left side of the container.
- `object-right`: This class positions the image on the right side of the container.
- `object-top`: This class positions the image at the top of the container.
- `object-bottom`: This class positions the image at the bottom of the container.

These classes can be combined with other TailwindCSS classes to further style the image by setting specific widths and heights, adding borders or shadows, or adjusting opacity.
