TailwindCSS offers several utility classes for working with the `<img>` tag. Here are some of the most commonly used ones: 
- `object-contain`: This class adjusts the image to fit within its container while maintaining its aspect ratio. It will shrink or enlarge the image as necessary to ensure it fits in the container. 
- `object-cover`: This class configures the image to cover the entire container while maintaining its aspect ratio. It will crop or reduce the image to ensure it completely covers the container. 
- `object-fill`: This class adjusts the image to fill the entire container, without maintaining its aspect ratio. This may cause the image to stretch or compress to fit the container. 
- `object-none`: This class adjusts the image to its natural size, without any resizing or cropping. 
- `object-center`: This class centers the image horizontally and vertically within the container. 
- `object-left`: This class aligns the image to the left within its container. 
- `object-right`: This class aligns the image to the right within its container. 
- `object-top`: This class aligns the image to the top of its container. 
- `object-bottom`: This class aligns the image to the bottom of its container.

These classes can be used in conjunction with other TailwindCSS classes to provide additional styling to your images, such as setting a specific width or height, adding borders or shadows, or adjusting opacity.
