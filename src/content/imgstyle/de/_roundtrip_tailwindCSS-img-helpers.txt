TailwindCSS offers several utility classes for working with the `<img>` tag. Here are some of the most commonly used: 
- `object-contain`: This class adjusts the image to fit within its container while preserving its aspect ratio. It will scale down or enlarge the image to ensure it fits in the container. 
- `object-cover`: This class adjusts the image to cover the entire container while preserving its aspect ratio. It will either crop or scale down the image to ensure it covers the container. 
- `object-fill`: This class adjusts the image to fill the entire container without maintaining its aspect ratio. This may cause the image to stretch or compress to fit the container. 
- `object-none`: This class leaves the image at its natural size, without resizing or cropping. 
- `object-center`: This class centers the image horizontally and vertically within its container. 
- `object-left`: This class aligns the image to the left within its container. 
- `object-right`: This class aligns the image to the right within its container. 
- `object-top`: This class aligns the image to the top in its container. 
- `object-bottom`: This class aligns the image to the bottom in its container.

These classes can be used in combination with other TailwindCSS classes to style your images further, such as setting specific width or height, adding margins or shadows, or adjusting opacity.
